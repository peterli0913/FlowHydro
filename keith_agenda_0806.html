A1 ⭐ R127 四撬块分体运输作为基准方案 ｜ Baseline the four-module split shipment
3D 正在定稿；四撬块技术可行，屋顶整体吊装并非必选项。另核实到：分撬可能不仅受进口电梯尺寸限制，海运运输本身也可能要求分撬（国内正在核实）。若成立，屋顶方案不再有成本优势。 The 3D layout is being finalised; the four-module split is technically feasible and a roof lift is not mandatory. We are also checking whether sea freight itself requires the split, independent of the lift dimensions — if so, the roof option loses its advantage.
Ask: Can we baseline the four-module split shipment with site assembly, keeping the roof-replacement assessment as a parallel option that does not block design?
A2 ⭐ R38 SE01 清洁度验证方式 ｜ SE01 cleanliness verification
反应柱与分离器操作压力高，无法配置视镜，目视检查不可行；我方以取样分析残留浓度验证。验收标准（残留限度、检测方法）取决于项目 Level 分级与英方法规，国内无法代定。 Sight glasses cannot be fitted on the high-pressure vessels, so visual verification is not feasible. We propose verifying by sampling and residue concentration analysis.
Ask: Is residue sampling analysis acceptable, and can SW site QA define the acceptance criteria and analytical method?
A3 ⭐ 清洗方案的定位与分层 ｜ Scope and structure of the cleaning philosophy
即将发出的清洗方案定位为3Q 验证之后、项目运行期间的清洗方案，不是 3Q 验证文件；3Q / 清洁验证属属地职责。方案按 CMB（反应前） 与 CMA（反应后） 分开编写；CMA 为「先卸催化剂 → 水洗 → 溶剂洗 → 吹干」，因高活性催化剂残留遇溶剂存在燃爆风险。溶剂种类只给指导原则。 The document covers routine cleaning during operation, after 3Q validation — validation itself remains a site responsibility. It is split into CMB (before reaction) and CMA (after reaction); CMA follows catalyst discharge → water flush → solvent wash → dry, because residual active catalyst with solvent presents a fire/explosion risk.
Ask: Are you content with this scope split and the responsibility boundary?
A4 NEW 喷淋球形式 ｜ Spray ball type
现选可拆卸旋转喷淋球，设备数据表已标注处理流量。理解此前顾虑是可拆卸件可能脱落，我方将说明防脱结构。此项须在设备定标前锁定，否则厂家无法按要求配置。 We have selected a removable rotating spray ball. We understand the earlier concern was that a removable head could drop off; we will explain the retention arrangement. This needs locking before the vessel order.
Ask: Removable head with positive retention, or fixed type?
A5 R121 SE01 垫片材质 ｜ SE01 gasket material
分离器已调整为 270 ℃ / 7.5 MPaG，原拟 316L 金属环垫。垫片与法兰同为 316L、硬度接近，存在密封不住的风险；建议改用不同金属（如铜质）。 With the separator at 270 °C / 7.5 MPaG we had proposed a 316L metallic ring gasket. Gasket and flange would both be 316L with similar hardness, so sealing may be unreliable.
Ask: Can we change to a dissimilar metal gasket (e.g. copper)?
