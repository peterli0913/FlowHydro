## Part 1 — 讲解词 ｜ Talking Script

> 建议 4–5 分钟，按四段讲。中文为内部口径，英文为可直接说出的表述。

### 开场：先给结论 ｜ Open with the answer

**中文**
先直接回答你们关心的问题：按现在这版计划，设备的可投用日期是 **2027 年 11 月中下旬**。这是完成现场 DQ/IQ/OQ 之后、试车开车完成的时间点。

**English**
Let me give you the answer first. On this revision the equipment reaches **beneficial use in the second half of November 2027**. That is after site DQ/IQ/OQ, with commissioning and start-up complete.

---

### 第二段：这版计划的结构 ｜ How the schedule is built

**中文**
整个计划分四个阶段。设计定稿到今年 9 月——核心是 9 月初的 HAZOP，以及 9 月初研发数据到位后确定反应器尺寸。采购与制造从今年 9 月到明年 3 月。工厂验收、CE 认证和海运是明年 3 月到 8 月。现场安装与确认是明年 8 月到 11 月。

**English**
The schedule has four phases. Design finalisation runs to September this year, anchored on the HAZOP in early September and the reactor size decision once the R&D data arrives. Procurement and fabrication run from September through to March next year. FAT, CE certification and sea freight run from March to August. Site installation and qualification run from August to November.

---

### 第三段：关键路径在哪 ｜ Where the critical path sits

**中文**
关键路径很清楚：**反应器尺寸 → 主设备采购 → 设备制造 → FAT → CE 认证 → 海运 → 现场安装**。这条链上任何一环延后，投用日期同步顺延。目前卡在最前面一环——反应器尺寸要等 9 月初的研发数据。

**English**
The critical path is straightforward: **reactor sizing → main equipment procurement → fabrication → FAT → CE certification → shipping → site installation**. Any slip on that chain moves beneficial use by the same amount. Today it is held at the very first link — the reactor size is waiting on R&D data due in early September.

---

### 第四段：与上一版的差异 ｜ Change against the last revision

**中文**
相对 7 月 29 日那版，投用日期后移约 **两个月**，从 2027 年 9 月底移到 11 月中下旬。HAZOP 日期没有变。后移主要来自三件事：反应器尺寸要等研发数据、采购顺序按真实依赖关系重排、厂家返资料的往复周期此前计入不足。

**English**
Against the 29 July revision, beneficial use moves out by about **two months**, from late September 2027 to mid-to-late November 2027. The HAZOP date is unchanged. The movement comes from three things: the reactor size waiting on R&D data, the procurement sequence being re-ordered to reflect real dependencies, and the vendor feedback loop having been under-counted previously.

---

### 收束 ｜ Close

**中文**
需要说明的是，这版计划把之前隐含的乐观假设去掉了，节点之间的依赖关系是按实际执行顺序排的。所以这是一个我们认为可以承诺的日期，而不是一个理想情况下的日期。

**English**
It is worth saying that this revision removes assumptions that were previously optimistic, and sequences the dependencies as they will actually be executed. So this is a date we believe we can commit to, rather than a best-case date.

## Part 2 — 预计提问与回答 ｜ Anticipated Questions

### Q1. 比上次推后了多少？｜ How much later is this than the last plan?

**中文**
投用从 2027 年 9 月第 3 周移到 11 月第 3 周，约 **两个月**。这两个月是一次性产生的，不是逐级累加：反应器尺寸和采购顺序造成的前段延后，被后续各阶段等量传递到末端。中间阶段本身的工期没有拉长。

**English**
Beneficial use moves from week 3 of September 2027 to week 3 of November 2027 — about **two months**. That two months arises once, at the front end, and is then carried through unchanged: the durations of the downstream phases themselves have not been extended.

---

### Q2. 为什么会推后？｜ Why has it moved?

按三条讲，从最实质到最技术。

**① 反应器尺寸要等研发数据（主因）**

**中文**
DN80 方案出现高温点，需要改为 DN65 或 DN50，而支撑该判断的研发实验数据 9 月初才能出来。反应器是主设备，尺寸不定就无法提交采购。如果先行询价、之后再改规格，价格随之变动，比价流程要整个重走，反而更慢。

**English**
A high-temperature point was found on the DN80 case, so the reactor must change to DN65 or DN50, and the R&D test data supporting that decision only arrives in early September. The reactor is main equipment — until the size is fixed it cannot be released for purchase. Issuing an enquiry first and changing the specification later would invalidate the pricing and force the whole comparison process to be repeated, which would be slower still.

**② 采购顺序按真实依赖关系重排**

**中文**
上一版计划里，采购、材料表和 FAT 与设计定版存在重叠。这次把顺序恢复成实际执行链条：设计定版 → HAZOP → 文件升版 → 3D 定版 → 采购信息确认 → 厂家返资料 → 材料表与制造图 → 制造 → FAT → CE 认证。特别是 FAT 必须在制造完成之后，整橇 CE 评定必须在撬块组装完成之后，这两点之前排得偏前。

**English**
In the previous revision, procurement, BOM and FAT overlapped the design freeze. We have restored the real execution chain: design freeze → HAZOP → document revision → 3D freeze → procurement confirmation → vendor returns → BOM and fabrication drawings → build → FAT → CE. In particular, FAT can only follow completion of fabrication, and whole-skid CE assessment can only follow skid assembly — both were previously scheduled too early.

**③ 厂家返资料的往复周期此前计入不足**

**中文**
询价、比价、厂家返资料、我方审查，这是数周量级的实际往复，不是一个瞬时动作。电缆清单、PLC 硬件图和 I/O 分配表都要等仪表厂家最终选型返回后才能定版——例如本安还是隔爆，要由厂家能力决定。

**English**
Enquiry, comparison, vendor document return and our own review is a real cycle of several weeks, not an instantaneous step. The cable schedule, PLC hardware drawings and I/O allocation cannot be frozen until instrument vendor selection returns — for example whether a device is intrinsically safe or Ex d depends on what the vendor can actually supply.

---

### Q3. 有没有补救措施？｜ What mitigation is in place?

**中文**
有五项，都已经反映在这版计划里，不是设想。

1. **并行准备两套反应器方案。** DN65 与 DN50 的设计条件同时准备，9 月数据一到即可定案，不再额外占用时间。
2. **非反应器设备提前走采购。** 主设备中只有反应器未定，其余设备、阀门、仪表的询价可先行启动，不等反应器。
3. **CE 资料准备与设计收尾并行。** 认证资料从 3D 定版起同步准备，认证机构选定后可直接进入评审阶段，不串行等待。
4. **文件提前发英方预览。** 清洗说明、控制说明、操作手册等在国内会审同时发英方，让反馈与内部升版并行，避免两轮串联。
5. **建立变更影响清单。** 针对 R24-CR01 尺寸变更逐项列出受影响的设备、仪表、管线、安全阀与文件，确保升版一次到位，不出现反复补漏。

**English**
Five, and all of them are already reflected in this revision rather than being proposals.

1. **Two reactor cases prepared in parallel.** Design conditions for both DN65 and DN50 are being progressed, so the decision can be taken the moment the September data lands, with no additional time.
2. **Non-reactor equipment released early.** The reactor is the only main equipment item still open, so enquiries for the remaining equipment, valves and instruments can start without waiting for it.
3. **CE documentation prepared in parallel with design close-out.** Certification documents are being assembled from the 3D freeze onward, so once the notified body is appointed the review phase starts immediately rather than sequentially.
4. **Documents shared with the UK ahead of formal issue.** Cleaning philosophy, control philosophy and the operating manual go to the UK while internal review is still running, so your comments and our revision happen in parallel instead of in two serial rounds.
5. **A change-impact register.** For the R24-CR01 size change we are listing every affected item of equipment, instrumentation, piping, relief device and document, so the re-issue is done once and nothing is missed.

---

### Q4. 还能不能压缩？｜ Can it be pulled back?

**中文**
可以压缩的空间主要在两处，但都不完全由我方决定。

- **运输方案**：如果整橇运输可行，英方现场无需拆装与重复 IQ/OQ，可复用约 90% 的 FAT 结果，现场段有压缩空间。目前受集装箱高度限制（普通柜 2.35 m、高柜约 2.5–2.69 m，而撬块最高约 3.6–3.7 m），需确认超尺寸承运方案。
- **文件评审往复**：如果英方能在收到文件后按约定窗口反馈，可以少走一轮升版。

反过来，需要提醒的下行风险是**高压密封选型**——270 ℃ 泄放工况加涉氢排除了多数常规垫片材料，目前正在联系专业密封厂家。这一项在设备下单关键路径上，若长期无解会影响制造启动。

**English**
There are two places where time could be recovered, but neither is entirely within our control.

- **Transport route.** If whole-skid shipment is feasible, your site avoids disassembly, reassembly and repeat IQ/OQ, and roughly 90% of the FAT results can be reused — that compresses the site phase. It is currently constrained by container height: a standard container is 2.35 m internally and a high-cube about 2.5–2.69 m, against a skid height of about 3.6–3.7 m, so an oversized carrier option needs confirming.
- **Document review turnaround.** If comments come back within an agreed window, we can avoid one revision cycle.

Conversely, the downside risk to flag is the **high-pressure sealing selection**. The 270 °C relief duty combined with hydrogen service rules out most conventional gasket materials, and we are approaching specialist sealing suppliers. That item sits on the critical path for placing equipment orders, and if it stays open it will affect the start of fabrication.

---

### Q5. HAZOP 会不会也推后？｜ Does HAZOP move as well?

**中文**
不会。HAZOP 仍在 9 月初，日期没有变。反应器从 DN80 改到 DN65 或 DN50 属于处理规模的调整，只要 P&ID 配置不变，系统的安全配置和风险等级不变——尺寸减小方向上危险性是降低的。因此尺寸未最终定案不影响 HAZOP 按期开展。

前提是 **3D 布置与控制说明须在 HAZOP 前与你们谈定**，这两项是目前仅剩的、可能引发重大设计变更的内容。我们的目标是 **HAZOP 只做一次**。

**English**
No. HAZOP remains in early September, unchanged. Moving the reactor from DN80 to DN65 or DN50 is a change of throughput scale; provided the P&ID configuration is unchanged, the safety configuration and the risk profile are unchanged — and reducing the size moves hazard in the safer direction. So the size decision not being final does not prevent HAZOP proceeding on time.

The condition is that the **3D layout and the control philosophy must be agreed with you before HAZOP** — they are the only two remaining items that could still trigger a significant design change. Our objective is to **run HAZOP once and only once**.

---

### Q6. 这个日期可靠吗？会不会再变？｜ How firm is this date?

**中文**
这版计划相对之前更保守，原因是把三类此前偏乐观的假设去掉了：设计与采购的重叠、厂家往复周期、FAT 与 CE 的先后顺序。目前挂着的不确定项有三个，我们会逐月更新：

1. 9 月初研发数据与反应器尺寸定案
2. 高压密封材质选型
3. 运输方案（分体 vs 整橇）与超尺寸承运可行性

前两项在 9 月至 10 月应可收敛；运输方案取决于双方共同决策。

**English**
This revision is more conservative than the previous one because it removes three optimistic assumptions: the overlap between design and procurement, the vendor turnaround loop, and the ordering of FAT against CE. Three uncertainties remain open, and we will update monthly:

1. the September R&D data and the reactor size decision;
2. the high-pressure sealing material selection;
3. the transport route (split versus whole skid) and the feasibility of an oversized carrier.

We expect the first two to converge during September and October; the transport route depends on a joint decision.
