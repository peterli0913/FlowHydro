A3 Scope and structure of the cleaning philosophy to be issued this week
Our output:
  • The document covers routine cleaning during project operation, after 3Q validation. It is not a 3Q validation document; validation remains a site responsibility.
  • It is split into CMB (before reaction) and CMA (after reaction).
  • CMB: wash with the reaction solvent, dry, then charge catalyst.
  • CMA: discharge catalyst under water and inert gas protection, water flush to remove catalyst fines, then solvent wash, then dry — residual active catalyst in contact with solvent presents a fire and explosion risk.
  • Solvent selection is given as a general principle only; the specific solvent follows the project chemistry.
  • The reactor can be cleaned separately; tanks and the separator can be cleaned on a circulation loop, filled to ensure full wetted contact.
Question Are you content with this scope, the CMB / CMA split and the responsibility boundary for validation?
Decision / action

A5 SE01 gasket material R121
Our position:
  • The separator is now at 270 °C design temperature and 7.5 MPaG design pressure; a 316L metallic ring gasket was originally proposed.
  • Gasket and flange would both be 316L with similar hardness, so sealing may be unreliable.
  • We propose a dissimilar metal gasket, for example copper.
