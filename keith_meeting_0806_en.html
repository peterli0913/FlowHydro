A3 清洗方案的定位与分层 Scope and structure of the cleaning philosophy 本周发出 / to be issued this week
我方输出 Our output：
  • 文件覆盖的是 3Q 验证之后、项目运行期间的常规清洗，不是 3Q 验证文件；验证仍属属地职责。 The document covers routine cleaning during project operation, after 3Q validation. It is not a 3Q validation document; validation remains a site responsibility.
  • 按 CMB（反应前）与 CMA（反应后）分开编写。 It is split into CMB (before reaction) and CMA (after reaction).
  • CMB：用反应溶剂清洗 → 吹干 → 装填催化剂。 CMB: wash with the reaction solvent, dry, then charge catalyst.
  • CMA：在注水与惰性气体保护下卸催化剂 → 水洗冲净催化剂粉末 → 溶剂清洗 → 吹干（高活性催化剂残留遇溶剂有燃爆风险）。 CMA: discharge catalyst under water and inert gas protection, water flush to remove catalyst fines, then solvent wash, then dry — residual active catalyst in contact with solvent presents a fire and explosion risk.
  • 溶剂种类只给指导原则，具体溶剂随项目化学体系定。 Solvent selection is given as a general principle only; the specific solvent follows the project chemistry.
  • 反应器可单独清洗；储罐与分离器可打循环清洗，须充满以保证润湿。 The reactor can be cleaned separately; tanks and the separator can be cleaned on a circulation loop, filled to ensure full wetted contact.
Question 问题 这个定位、CMB / CMA 分层，以及验证责任边界，是否可接受？ Are you content with this scope, the CMB / CMA split and the responsibility boundary for validation?

A5 SE01 垫片材质 SE01 gasket material R121
我方立场 Our position：
  • 分离器现为设计温度 270 ℃、设计压力 7.5 MPaG；原拟采用 316L 金属环垫。 The separator is now at 270 °C design temperature and 7.5 MPaG design pressure; a 316L metallic ring gasket was originally proposed.
  • 垫片与法兰同为 316L、硬度接近，存在密封不住的风险。 Gasket and flange would both be 316L with similar hardness, so sealing may be unreliable.
  • 建议改用不同金属垫片，例如铜质。 We propose a dissimilar metal gasket, for example copper.
Question 问题 能否改用不同金属垫片以保证密封？ Can we change to a dissimilar metal gasket to secure the seal?
