A-6. CE 认证范围澄清 — 不需要单独 LVD
原议题 R21 (05/06) · 关闭 05/12 · 负责 赵子亮
整撬走 CE-MD（机械指令）认证即可，不需要单独再做 CE-LVD（低压电气指令）。 因为 MD 是综合性指令，已涵盖电气风险；同一产品也不能既算机械又算电气。
A-6. CE certification scope — LVD not required
Item R21 (05/06) · Closed 05/12 · Owner: Zhao Ziliang
The whole skid undergoes CE-MD (Machinery Directive) certification only. A separate CE-LVD (Low-Voltage Directive) is not required because MD is comprehensive and already covers electrical risks; one product cannot be both a machine and an electrical-only device.
